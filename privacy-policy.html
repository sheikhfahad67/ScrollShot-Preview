ScrollShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

ScrollShot is a Chrome extension that captures screenshots of web pages and lets you edit and save them.

What we collect

Nothing. ScrollShot does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data or browsing data.

How your screenshots are handled

  • A screenshot is created on your computer only when you start a capture.
  • It is held temporarily in your browser so the popup and the editor tab can show it. It is replaced by the next capture, or removed when you choose New capture.
  • Edits and exports (PNG, JPG, PDF, clipboard) happen on your computer. Files are saved only where you choose.
  • ScrollShot never uploads screenshots or page content to any server.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to capture and scroll the tab you choose.
  • storage: to show the progress and result of the current capture. It is kept in your browser for the session only and is cleared when the browser closes.
  • Access to all sites: so pages that keep their content in frames from other sites can be captured. It is used only when you start a capture.

Third parties, analytics, ads

None. ScrollShot makes no network requests and uses no analytics, ads or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
